Home, in the making

Lokesh · Artist statement

I started this project with the idea that home might be in the little things. I chose to look closely at my dorm room: what I brought with me, what I bought for college, and what changed once I started living here.

I made an interactive website around my desk, wardrobe, and bed. The models combine phone scans and photographs with work in Blender and models from BlenderKit. Visitors can rotate the furniture, open the desk drawer, and select objects to read about them. I wanted people to look around and choose what caught their attention.

My dad’s cap and my sister’s iPad came from family. The suitcase under my bed was bought for trips to India, where I was born. Other things are connected to being at Virginia Tech, like the portfolio that held my first résumé and the stickers from my first in-person hackathon. Some objects, like the hand sanitizer, are simply things I use.

Looking through the room made me think more about the experiences attached to these belongings. I expected to use my Mac most, but engineering work changed that. My roommate suggested the fan and influenced my choice of pillow. My idea of home now includes the habits I’m developing here and the person I share the room with. The things from my family still belong in that picture. This project shows how those parts of my life are coming together halfway through the semester.
